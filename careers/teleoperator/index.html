Teleoperator

Redwood City, CA · Full-time or part-time
Quebec City · Part-time

The role

This is a founding-cohort role for someone who wants to go deep. You'll pilot robots across a growing range of tasks - inspection, grading, and authentication; picking, packing, and sorting; returns processing; and kitting and light-assembly work - and help define what "great teleoperation" looks like as the role and tooling are still being built.

What you'll do

  • Pilot robots across a range of evolving, sometimes unscripted tasks.
  • Detect, troubleshoot, and recover from errors live, without a script to follow.
  • Collect and annotate data from piloting sessions to support model training.
  • Evaluate ML model performance and report findings back to the engineering team.
  • Contribute to a shared library of SOPs - turning what you learn piloting each new task into documentation the next operator, and the next robot, builds on.
  • Help us think through better ways for the robot to perform tasks and structure workflows.
  • Work with Claude Code and GitHub as part of the team's day-to-day tooling.

What we're looking for

  • Full use of both hands and arms; excellent hand-eye coordination and manual dexterity.
  • A track record of reliable, high-quality output in a role where consistency matters - not just occasional strong performance.
  • Comfort giving structured, specific feedback on system or robot behavior. You'll be a direct feedback loop into how the tech gets better, not just an end user of it.
  • Ability to operate with minimal supervision across evolving, sometimes unscripted tasks, and to exercise good judgment about when to flag something up.
  • Generally tech-savvy - comfortable picking up new tools and software quickly. You'll work with things like GitHub and AI coding assistants such as Claude Code. No coding background required, just someone who isn't thrown off by unfamiliar software.
  • Reliable on-site or hybrid availability, with some flexibility for occasional travel to support new deployments.
  • No robotics background required. We've hired people from competitive gaming, esports, and other high-precision skill backgrounds.
