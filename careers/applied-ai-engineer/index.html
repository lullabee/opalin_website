Senior Applied AI Engineer, Robotics

Paris, France · Full-time

Your role

  • Design and build AI and data pipelines for robotics: data ingestion, storage, transformation, labeling, training, evaluation, and deployment.
  • Work with multimodal robot data: images, video, trajectories, actions, task sequences, operator inputs, and execution outcomes.
  • Contribute to learning systems that improve manipulation reliability in complex, variable, real-world environments.
  • Develop and fine-tune models for perception, action, task understanding, and robot behavior.
  • Build evaluation loops that connect model performance to real robot execution, not just offline benchmarks.
  • Work closely with robotics software engineers to deploy, test, debug, and improve AI components on physical systems.

What you bring

  • 5+ years in applied AI, machine learning, robotics, computer vision, or closely related fields.
  • Strong Python required. PyTorch experience expected.
  • Experience building production-grade data or ML pipelines, from raw data to deployed models.
  • Solid understanding of deep learning, computer vision, and multimodal models.
  • Familiarity with supervised, self-supervised, reinforcement learning, imitation learning, or learning from demonstration.
  • Practical experience working in collaborative software teams, using tools such as Docker, Git, CI/CD, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Bonus: robotics experience, VLM/VLA models, manipulation, teleoperation data, simulation, Kubernetes, or deployment on edge and robotic systems.

Why OPALIN

  • A founding team that has shipped before and knows what to avoid.
  • A rare opportunity to build the data and learning infrastructure behind real-world robotic intelligence.
  • A direct line between the models you train, the data you structure, and the robots that operate in production.

We value engineers who can turn messy real-world data into systems that improve over time. If you do not check every box but think you can make a meaningful impact, apply.
