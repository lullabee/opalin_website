About Opalin

Robotics for environments where reliability matters.

Opalin builds robotic workstations that take on the repetitive, variable parts of manual work in manufacturing and logistics. These are the parts that full automation never made sense for. Our first product, Opalin One, learns a new task in as little as 48 hours. It needs only power and an internet connection, and it works inside the process your team already runs.

Built for the floor, not the demo.

For decades, automation meant rigid machines that did one job and broke the moment reality went off script. Real workflows are messy and full of edge cases. We think the next era of robotics belongs to systems that show up every day and simply work, without an army of integrators re-solving the same problem for every customer.

What we believe

01

Function over form

A robot should be defined by the job it does, not the shape it takes. We start from the task.

02

Deployments over demos

The hardest work lives where hardware meets software, and where a lab pilot has to survive a real Tuesday on a real warehouse floor.

03

People and robots, together

Where full automation doesn't pencil out, the answer isn't a robot instead of a person. It's both, working the same task, each doing the part they're best at.

Who we are

Opalin's founding team has built and scaled robotics from the inside at some of the companies that defined the field: autonomous vehicles at Google, self-driving trucks as co-founder of Otto and through its acquisition by Uber, and the robotics platform at NVIDIA that other companies build on. Opalin is what comes next — built on the lessons of getting robots out of the lab and into the real world. Our team works across Redwood City, Quebec, and France.
