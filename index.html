Opalin One

A new hire for your repetitive work.

Opalin One is a compact robotic workstation. You show it the work, it does the work, and it gets better at the work over time, right inside the process your team already runs.

Not automation the old way

Traditional automation asks you to rebuild your operation around a machine: fixed scripts, long integrations and a system that breaks when a new SKU shows up. Opalin One works the other way around. It learns your task on your floor, with your definition of what "done right" looks like.
