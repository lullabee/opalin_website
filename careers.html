Senior Robotics Software Engineer

Build robotics software that holds up in real-world deployment, not just in demos.

Opalin builds physical intelligence for operators running reliable robots in the real world. Our founding team brings 50+ years of combined experience in robotics, autonomous vehicles, ML, NVIDIA, Google, and Uber. Four startups shipped. We have seen what breaks after the demo, and we build for deployment.

Your role

  • Design and develop robust robotics software components: motion, planning, navigation, and execution on real robots, not just in simulation.
  • Build pipelines that hold under real terrain constraints: latency, sensor noise, dynamic obstacles, and operational uncertainty.
  • Implement fault recovery logic and diagnostic tooling that make the difference between a demo and a deployment.
  • Work end-to-end: prototype, validate in simulation, and stabilize on physical hardware.
  • Contribute to software architecture and code quality on a team that moves fast and builds to last.

What you bring

  • 5-10 years in robotics software development, with a focus on planning, control, and/or navigation.
  • Strong Python required. C++ is a plus.
  • ROS/ROS2 experience and integration on complex physical systems.
  • Solid grasp of trajectory planning algorithms, navigation, and motion control fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with simulation environments like Gazebo, Unity, or equivalent.
  • Bonus: safety-critical systems, real-time constraints, numerical optimization, and advanced calibration.

Why Opalin

A founding team that has shipped before and knows what to avoid.

A direct line between what you build and what runs in production.

A formative moment: building software infrastructure that keeps systems running beyond the pilot.

We value engineers who build on real robots, not perfect CVs. If you do not check every box but think you can make a meaningful impact, apply.

Location: Paris, with occasional travel within France, Europe, and the US.
